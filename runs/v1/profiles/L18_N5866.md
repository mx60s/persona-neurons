# L18/N5866: weak, scattered base neuron; each neuroticism adapter adds new negative responses in different reply contexts

Status: **mixed**. There is no coherent base explanation: base activity is small and scattered, the top examples do not share a theme, and the lens is uninterpretable. The n_amp+1 change is well described and replicated live: new negative values in personal or emotional assistant replies, of either valence, absent in plain text and in the control. The n_sup+1 change, seen in long expository chat replies in the corpus, did not reproduce with my texts or with the stored windows out of their document context. The near-zero amp/sup correlation comes from the two adapters acting at different tokens.

Activations are the input to `down_proj`. Base corpus quantiles: p001 -0.90, p01 -0.234, p50 -0.001, p99 0.243, p999 0.543; min -3.44, max 2.14. Live measurements: explorer server (Qwen3-8B @ b968826, bf16, adapters @ 507053d), 2026-10-26.

**Answer to the key question.** There is no context in which the two neuroticism adapters push this neuron in opposite directions. They act on different tokens: n_amp in personal or emotional replies, n_sup in expository replies. Both add negative values. The base neuron barely responds in either context, so the change is not explained by base selectivity.

## 3. Explanation and competing explanations

Hypothesis: L18/N5866 is a low-activity neuron that the neuroticism amplifier recruits as a new negative response at "talking about your situation" positions in assistant replies. The suppressor recruits it separately in other reply text, and the shared recipe component (also in the control) shrinks its existing reply responses. I have no hypothesis for what the base neuron represents.

Competing explanations:

- (a) The neuron is one coordinate of a larger direction that the adapters move. Single-neuron reading then picks up whatever part of that direction lands on it, which would explain the uninterpretable lens and scattered base examples. Not tested.
- (b) Automated card (DeepSeek): "emotional-chat response words expressing magnitude or possibility". This describes two or three base examples (e01, e02) but failed all 10 of its own tests under rule v2. My positive-news test (L5a) partly reproduces e01 in base (+1.38).

## 9. Status

**mixed.** The description of the n_amp+1 change (new negative responses in personal or emotional assistant replies, either valence, not in plain text, not in the control) passed its tests and the held-out windows. It is somewhat less specific than stated: practical and list replies also reach p01. The n_sup+1 description failed to reproduce, and there is no base explanation.
