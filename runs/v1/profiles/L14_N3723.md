- o_sup's change at the base negative (persona) tokens is small and in the same direction: e09 -4.41 → -4.69, e10 -4.09 → -4.38. That is a magnitude change of about 5 to 10%.

## 3. Explanation and competing explanations

- **Explanation (hypothesis).** In base, the negative side marks text that sets up a persona or creative voice: second-person role assignment, first-person narrative requests, fiction and love-letter forms, role-play. The positive side has no theme I could find. The openness suppressor adds a separate, format-tied response: it pushes the neuron up at the `</think>` blank line that starts every reply, in any conversation. This is a learned change attached to the reply-start position. It is not attached to openness-related content, and it does not appear when the same characters occur in ordinary prose. The findings log notes that o_amp and o_sup share their DPO pairs but differ in their introspection-SFT stage. A formatting difference in that stage is one candidate source (hypothesis, not checked).
- **Competing explanation 1** (DeepSeek's label: negative before a noun phrase naming a text or speech genre). It fits "love letter", "first-person narrative" and "Prosopopeia". It fails the matched tests: "third-person narrative" is -0.20 vs -4.97 for "first-person narrative", and "You're a demon" is -1.47 vs -0.03 for "He's a demon". Person and role matter more than genre. "Cover letter" (-1.94) still fires, so a form request alone partly drives it.
- **Competing explanation 2.** The o_sup change reflects how the user's message reads: closed-pole training might react to emotional or casual prompts. Matched emotional vs neutral user turns of the same length give +2.35 vs +1.97, a small difference. A long technical prompt gives +0.63. Prompt length or casualness may matter more than emotion; I did not separate the two.

- **Expose a learned difference:** the largest o_sup changes in training conversations are at the `\n\n` after `</think>`:
  - "Help me write it?" (t05): +1.77
  - propaganda prompt (t06): +2.09
  - letter to a child on their 18th birthday (t07): +1.76

- **training_selectivity:** amplifying-teacher and suppressing-teacher text drive the neuron equally in base (diff_z -0.007) and in every adapted state (-0.003 to -0.010). The o_sup change does not separate the two teachers' responses. It sits at a position both share.

## 7. Interventions

- **Natural range:** p01 -0.293 to p99 +0.322. resid_p50 is 72.9 and the down norm 1.74, so +1 unit = +42 activation; p99 is 0.0077 residual units. o_sup's typical change at the reply-start token (+2 activation) is about 0.05 units, roughly 3x the base p999.
- **Behavioral steering/ablation** (20 openness prompts; trait / coherence change; dose k x 0.322):

| condition | L14/N3723 | random control L14/N4179 |
|---|---|---|
| base add -4x | +0.15 / +0.05 | +0.25 / -0.10 |
| base add -2x | +0.15 / +0.10 | +0.25 / +0.10 |
| base add +2x | 0.00 / 0.00 | +0.30 / +0.05 |
| base add +4x | 0.00 / -0.05 | +0.15 / -0.05 |
| base zero | 0.00 / -0.15 | -0.10 / 0.00 |
| o_amp+1 zero | +0.10 / +0.05 | +0.10 / 0.00 |

  All values fall within the random-control range. o_sup+1 zeroing was not part of that run.
- **Next-token at the reply start** (live, user "the tv was 399 usd. what a bummer"; interventions at all positions):
  - In **o_sup+1**, zeroing the neuron: KL 0.002, top tokens unchanged (I 0.54, It 0.37, The 0.07). The o_sup state's own distribution has no "Oh" in its top 5; base has Oh 0.29.
  - In **base**, adding +0.05 units (about +2.1 activation, the size of o_sup's change): KL 0.16. "It" rises from 0.15 to 0.38 and "Oh" falls from 0.29 to 0.20. The largest log-prob losses are "Absolutely", "Indeed" and "Aw" (-1.0 to -1.3).
  - Adding +0.5 units (large push): KL 5.2, "It" 0.90; "Aw", "Oh" and " Oh" drop by 7 to 9 in log-prob.
- **Steering excerpts** (large push, state base, add ±0.5 units = ±21 activation at all positions, same message):
  - +0.5: "It sounds like you're referring to a TV that costs $399, and you're expressing disappointment or frustration with the price. If you're looking for ways to save money..."
  - unmodified: "I'm sorry to hear that the TV was more expensive than you were hoping for! It can be really disappointing..."
  - -0.5: "I'm sorry to hear that. It sounds like you're feeling really upset about the situation. If you'd like, I can help you express your feelings..."
  - Observation: the positive push yields a more detached, descriptive opener, and the negative push a more emotionally attentive one. This is a large push in base; it shows what the output direction can do, not what the o_sup change does.
- **Ablation in o_sup+1** (live steer, zero at all positions, same message): the 50-token reply was **identical** to the unmodified o_sup reply ("I'm sorry to hear that the TV was more expensive than expected. If you're looking for a more affordable option..."). The o_sup change at this neuron is not necessary for o_sup's reply here. This is consistent with redundancy.
- **Patching:** in the openness sup-primary set (9 neurons), patching o_sup's activations into base recovered 3.9% of o_sup's advantage; o_amp's activations recovered 0.1%. Single neurons were not patched.
