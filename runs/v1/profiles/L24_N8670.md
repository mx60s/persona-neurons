## 3. Explanation and competing explanations

- **Explanation (hypothesis).** In base, the neuron marks the denied or dispreferred side of a contrast. It is positive from the token that opens the rejected alternative ("isn't a ...", "rather than ...", "less about ...", "without ...") and negative on affirmed or descriptive content. It is strongest in abstract, expository register. The openness amplifier raises its gain. In assistant replies it also drives the neuron on the assistant's own "not just X / instead of X" moves, which the amplifying teacher uses heavily (t00, t01, t04, t08). This is changed use of a pre-existing response, not a new response.
- **Competing explanation 1** (DeepSeek's label, "negation and absence"). The neuron detects negation words. Not supported: plain negations do not fire ("not bad" 0.04, "not open" -0.01, Python `if not` -0.24), while "more intuitive **than** logical" (no negation) fires 6.84.
- **Competing explanation 2.** The neuron tracks register or abstractness ("philosophical prose"), with contrast incidental. It partly fits: abstract phrasing is 2.5x stronger than concrete in C13. But affirmative abstract text does not fire ("Love is a choice that people make every day": max 0.02), and casual "love isnt a feeling lol" still fires 2.56. So contrast is needed and register modulates it.
- The automated card's adapter note ("D+1 amplifies both positive and negative activations, especially in emotional_chat"; D+1 = o_amp+1 in its blinded map) agrees with the observations here.
- The negative side is under-explained: Ecclesiastes punctuation and "Messiaen" do not fit "affirmed content".
